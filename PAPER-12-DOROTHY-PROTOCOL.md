A theory that is not falsifiable is not science; it is dogma. This paper moves the Logos Unified Field framework from a philosophical and mathematical model to a testable scientific theory. We present the "Dorothy Protocol," a comprehensive, pre-registered experimental program designed to empirically validate the core claim of a conscious, participatory cosmos. This is not a search for anomalies; it is a targeted, scientific program to prove we're not in the "Kansas" of materialism anymore.

## 3. Experimental Design: The Modern Double-Slit

While the protocol can be applied to various systems (like Random Number Generators), the primary proposed experiment is a modernized version of the classic double-slit experiment.

### Setup:
A standard double-slit apparatus with a single-photon source and a high-resolution detector array capable of measuring interference visibility (a metric for how "wavelike" the results are).

### The Variable:
A human subject (or a group) trained in techniques of deep focus (e.g., meditation, focused prayer). The subject's physiological state (EEG for brainwave coherence, HRV for heart-rate variability) will be continuously monitored to provide a quantitative proxy for "coherent intention."

### The Task:
The experiment will be run in randomized, double-blind blocks:
- **"Wave" blocks:** Subject intends for photons to behave as waves (creating clear interference pattern)
- **"Particle" blocks:** Subject intends for photons to behave as particles (creating two distinct clumps with no interference)

### The Prediction:
We predict a statistically significant increase in interference visibility during "Wave" blocks and a decrease during "Particle" blocks, correlated with the subject's measured physiological coherence.

## 4. The Statistical Threshold: 6-Sigma

The history of mind-matter research is plagued by claims of small effects that are difficult to replicate. To overcome this, the Dorothy Protocol requires an unprecedented level of statistical rigor.

We are not looking for a "statistically significant" result. We are pre-declaring a specific, extraordinary threshold for confirmation:

**Confirmation Threshold:** A cumulative result of 6-sigma (p<10⁻⁹, or less than one in a billion odds of being due to chance) across a pre-registered series of at least ten independent, successful replications in different laboratories.

This is a higher bar than the 5-sigma threshold used to confirm the discovery of the Higgs boson. Meeting this standard would be scientifically undeniable.
